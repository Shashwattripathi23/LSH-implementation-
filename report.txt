Exact Jaccard Similarity Verification (Alessa)

- Implemented exact Jaccard similarity verification for candidate user pairs generated by the LSH algorithm.
- Loaded the user–movie matrix and candidate pairs.
- Extracted the rated-movie sets for the users involved in the candidate pairs.
- Calculated Jaccard similarity using the intersection and union of users' rated-movie sets.
- Applied the strict threshold:- Jaccard similarity > 0.5.
- Verified 396,141 candidate pairs; all 396,141 pairs were unique.
- Found 1,200 qualifying pairs with Jaccard similarity greater than 0.5.
- Saved the final verified pairs in `result.txt`, with the format `u1,u2` and `u1 < u2`.
- Total verification runtime: 24.80 seconds.
